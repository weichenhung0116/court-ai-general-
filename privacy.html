COURT.AI 隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 11 月 4 日

COURT.AI 籃球戰術後設認知鷹架（以下稱「本平台」）是大學籃球課程使用的教學網站，網址為 https://weichenhung0116.github.io/court-ai/。 本平台是純前端網頁，沒有自己的伺服器或資料庫，不會把你的資料傳送或保存到本平台。

一、Google 帳號登入

本平台使用 Google 登入確認使用者身分。登入後取得的姓名與電子郵件只會在你的瀏覽器中，用來顯示登入狀態與判斷是否為授課教師，不會被傳送或保存到任何其他地方。

二、Google 雲端硬碟

  • 本平台只申請 drive.file 權限，只能存取由本平台建立的檔案與資料夾，無法讀取你雲端硬碟中的其他任何檔案。
  • 你上傳的比賽影片會直接從你的瀏覽器存進你自己的 Google 雲端硬碟，存放在以「班別_組別_姓名」命名的資料夾中；你繳交的表現紀錄表（CSV）與知識族譜也會放在同一個資料夾。
  • 為了繳交作業，該資料夾會以「檢視者」權限分享給授課教師。
  • 雲端硬碟授權約一小時後自動失效；你也可以隨時到 Google 帳戶的第三方存取權設定移除本平台的授權。

三、儲存在你瀏覽器中的資料

以下資料只會存在你目前使用的這台裝置的瀏覽器（localStorage）中，不會上傳到任何伺服器：

  • 你選擇的班別、組別、姓名與小組隊長、3 對 3 角色設定，以及中／英文語言設定
  • 球員表現紀錄表（得分、籃板、助攻、失誤等，以及隊伍遇到的問題）
  • AI 助教對話紀錄（由你自行從 ChatGPT 複製貼上）
  • 知識族譜與後設認知評估結果（由 ChatGPT 依你的對話產生，你貼回本平台後只存在這台裝置）
  • 繳交紀錄與各節後設認知分數（用來顯示「07 成績」的個人進度）
  • 雲端硬碟資料夾編號（用來避免重複建立資料夾）

清除瀏覽器資料即可刪除這些資料；對話紀錄也可以在 AI 助教頁面按「清除這台裝置的對話紀錄」刪除。

四、分組名單

賽程頁面會顯示各組同學的姓名，讓同學找到自己的組別與輪替順序；不會顯示學號或系級。

五、第三方服務

  • Google（登入、雲端硬碟）：依 Google 隱私權政策處理。
  • YouTube：戰術影片以 YouTube 嵌入播放（按下播放後才會載入），YouTube 可能依其政策使用 Cookie。
  • ChatGPT：本平台不會自動把任何內容傳給 ChatGPT。只有在你自己複製提示詞、收尾指令或合併分析指令並貼到 ChatGPT 時，內容才會依 OpenAI 的政策處理。

六、聯絡方式

若對本政策有任何疑問，請聯絡授課教師：weichenlovejesus@gmail.com

COURT.AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 4, 2026

COURT.AI, a metacognitive scaffold for basketball tactics (the "Platform"), is a teaching website for a university basketball course, available at https://weichenhung0116.github.io/court-ai/. The Platform runs entirely in your browser and has no server or database of its own, so it does not send your data to, or store it on, the Platform.

1. Google sign-in

The Platform uses Google sign-in to confirm who you are. Your name and email stay in your browser. They are used only to show your sign-in status and to check whether you are the teacher, and are not sent or stored anywhere else.

2. Google Drive

  • The Platform requests only the drive.file permission, so it can access only the files and folders it created and cannot read anything else in your Drive.
  • Videos you upload go directly from your browser to your own Google Drive, in a folder named "class_group_name". The stat sheets (CSV) and knowledge maps you submit go in the same folder.
  • To submit your work, this folder is shared with your teacher with "Viewer" access.
  • Drive access expires after about one hour. You can also remove the Platform's access at any time in your Google Account third-party access settings.

3. Data stored in your browser

The following data is kept only in the browser (localStorage) of the device you are using and is not uploaded to any server:

  • Your class, group, name and group captain, your 3-on-3 role, and your language setting
  • Player stat sheets (points, rebounds, assists, turnovers and so on, plus the problems your team faced)
  • AI coach chat logs (which you copy from ChatGPT and paste in yourself)
  • Knowledge maps and metacognitive evaluations (created by ChatGPT from your conversation and kept on this device after you paste them in)
  • Your submission history and metacognition score for each session (to show your progress in 07 Grades)
  • Your Drive folder ID (to avoid creating duplicate folders)

Clearing your browser data deletes all of this. You can also delete chat logs on the AI Coach page with "Clear chat logs on this device".

4. Group rosters

The schedule page shows students' names so that students can find their group and rotation. It does not show student IDs or departments.

5. Third-party services

  • Google (sign-in, Drive): handled under the Google Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube: tactics videos are embedded from YouTube (they load only after you press play), and YouTube may use cookies under its own policies.
  • ChatGPT: the Platform never sends anything to ChatGPT automatically. Content is handled under OpenAI's policies only when you copy a prompt, wrap-up command or combined analysis command and paste it into ChatGPT yourself.

6. Contact

If you have questions about this policy, please contact your teacher: weichenlovejesus@gmail.com
